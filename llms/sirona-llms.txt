# Sirona Medical

> Radiologists get more done with Sirona. Reporting, viewing, PACS, worklist, and AI unified in one cloud-native platform — the Radiology Operating System. Sirona Medical builds RadOS, a cloud-native platform that replaces the fragmented stack of legacy radiology IT (separate PACS, reporter, viewer, and worklist) with one integrated system. It is an FDA Class I device platform, SOC 2 Type II certified, and HIPAA compliant.

## Platform

- [RadOS — The Radiology Operating System](https://sironamedical.com/platform/rados): RadOS unifies all your clinical data and workflow software — reporter, PACS, viewer, worklist — in one cloud-native radiology platform built for agentic AI.
- [Reporter](https://sironamedical.com/platform/reporter): The first enterprise-grade cloud-native radiology reporter, the only FDA-regulated one. Migrate templates from PowerScribe or Fluency and report faster with AI.
- [Diagnostic Viewer](https://sironamedical.com/platform/viewer): A browser-based diagnostic viewer with Netflix-style edge streaming. Load images reliably from anywhere on earth — no workstations, no installs, powered by AI.
- [Worklist](https://sironamedical.com/platform/worklist): Run your practice from a single pane of glass. Sirona's intelligent worklist unifies volume across sites with AI patient jackets and customizable workflows.
- [Platform AI](https://sironamedical.com/platform/ai): Sirona's platform AI powers every app: auto-impressions, hanging protocols, focus mode, quality assist, and AI orchestration for third-party radiology AI.
- [PACS Archive](https://sironamedical.com/platform/pacs-archive): Radiology's first cloud-native PACS archive: millisecond retrieval, 11 nines of durability, managed migration, and no servers — at a fraction of legacy cost.
- [Amplify SDK](https://sironamedical.com/platform/sdk): Sirona's vision for opening the platform — a path for developers, AI vendors, and practices to build custom AI applications on unified data and unified events.
- [Zero-Footprint](https://sironamedical.com/platform/capability/zero-footprint): No workstation management, no installs, invisible upgrades every two weeks. Sirona runs in the browser on any computer with Chrome — zero-footprint radiology.
- [Integrations — DICOM, HL7 & FHIR](https://sironamedical.com/platform/capability/interoperability): Sirona integrates cleanly with your existing PACS, RIS, EMR, and worklist. Fully managed DICOM, HL7, and FHIR integration — no custom bridges, no lock-in.
- [PACS Data Migration](https://sironamedical.com/platform/migration): Move petabytes easier. How Sirona migrates data from legacy PACS archives with managed tooling, AWS Snowball, and a services team that has seen it all before.
- [Training & Onboarding](https://sironamedical.com/platform/training): How Sirona trains radiologists, technologists, and IT teams for a fast, low-disruption go-live — simplified training and change management for every role.
